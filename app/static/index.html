Better data makes a safer decision.

Record at 100–200 Hz for at least 30 seconds and include one complete stop. Keep the phone anywhere in the cabin or on the body; the magnitude transform removes orientation dependence.

01Record continuously inside the vehicle.
02Include an idle or near-idle period.
03Treat low-quality results as inconclusive.

This model detects combustion-engine vibration. Hybrids, engine auto-stop, loud audio, fans, road vibration, and unseen devices can affect results.
