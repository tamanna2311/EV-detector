What the model sees

One signal. Three layers of evidence.

The detector looks for combustion vibration; it does not directly identify a vehicle model or guarantee that a vehicle is electric.

01 Combine orientation

X, Y and Z become one orientation-independent acceleration magnitude.

02 Find stable peaks

Overlapping FFT windows focus on persistent vibration above 19 Hz.

03 Return uncertainty

Low-quality and out-of-distribution recordings can be marked inconclusive.

!
Interpret the result carefully

Hybrids, engine auto-stop, loud audio, fans, road vibration, phones outside a vehicle and unseen devices can affect the result. “No combustion signal detected” is not proof that a vehicle is electric.
